A small agency that does one thing only

We started in 2016 in via Garibaldi, and since then we have done one thing only: group travel. No ticketing, no last-minute cruises, no shop-window packages. Groups.

The first trip was a pilgrimage to Lourdes with forty-two people from a parish in Monza. The coach left at four in the morning and we got on too, because we wanted to see with our own eyes what worked and what did not. Since then every group leaves with a tour leader of ours, and that is not a marketing choice: it is how we learned to work.

In ten years 640 groups have left. We learned that the things that ruin a trip are always the same — a room on the fifth floor with no lift, a lunch scheduled when the group is still full from breakfast, a balance asked for the day before — and we removed them one by one.

Today there are five of us: someone who prepares the quotes, someone who deals with the hotels, someone who follows the accounts and two tour leaders who between them speak four languages. The phone in the office is always answered by a person.

How we work

First you count, then you sell

The price comes from the real costs: coach, hotels, guides, entrance fees. If the numbers do not add up we say so, instead of cutting quality.

A group is not a crowd

We know who walks slowly, who cannot eat gluten, who wants to sit at the front of the coach. It is written on the travel sheet.

The paperwork in order

Licence, liability insurance, guarantee fund. For schools we prepare the complete file for the tender.
