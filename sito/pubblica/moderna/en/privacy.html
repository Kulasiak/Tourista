Privacy notice

Updated 1 September 2026

Who handles your data

Urania, via Garibaldi 42, 20121 Milan, Italy, VAT no. IT09876543210. For any question: gruppi@touristaviaggi.it.

What data we collect

What you write in the contact form (name, phone, email, organisation, notes about the trip) and, if you then travel with us, the data needed for the booking: date of birth, identity document, any dietary or mobility needs.

Why we use it

To prepare your quote, to organise the trip (passing hotels and suppliers only what they need) and to meet legal, tax and insurance obligations.

For how long

Quote requests with no follow-up: two years. Data from trips actually taken: ten years, as accounting obligations require.

Who we share it with

Only those needed to make you travel: hotels, transport companies, local agents, the insurer. Never for third-party advertising, never sold.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see, correct or delete your data, and object to its use. Just write to gruppi@touristaviaggi.it: we answer within thirty days.

Cookies

This site uses no profiling cookies and has no advertising trackers. Only the technical cookies needed to make it work remain.
