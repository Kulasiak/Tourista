Coach or plane: when each one is worth it

The real arithmetic, with numbers: what each solution really costs and how it changes with the size of the group.

Tourista Viaggi · 2026-05-08 · 4 min read

It is the first question we are asked, and the answer depends on three things: how many you are, how far it is and how many days you have.

A coach is a fixed cost. A coach with two drivers costs between €600 and €800 a day, divided among the participants. With fifty people that is €12–16 each per day; with twenty-five it becomes €24–32. Below twenty-five participants a dedicated coach almost never pays off.

A plane is a cost per person. The ticket costs the same whether you are twenty or fifty (indeed, above twenty you get better group fares). Nothing is divided: it is multiplied.

The arithmetic of distance. Up to eight hours on the road the coach almost always wins, and it also takes you everywhere once you arrive, with no local rentals. Beyond twelve hours the coach costs two days of travelling: two days of hotel and driver's wages that the plane does not have.

Time is money for you too. A group that leaves for five days and spends two of them on the motorway has had a three-day trip and paid for five. For Prague, eleven hours away, it works because you travel overnight; for Lisbon it does not.

The rule of thumb. Under eight hours: coach. Between eight and twelve: coach if you travel overnight and stay at least four days. Beyond that: plane, and hire a coach on site for the transfers.

In every quote we send you will find the arithmetic done both ways, with the real numbers of your group. So you choose, not us.
