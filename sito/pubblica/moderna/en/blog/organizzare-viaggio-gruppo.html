How to organise a group trip without surprises

From the first idea to departure: what to do first, what to ask the agency and the four mistakes that cost the most.

Tourista Viaggi · 2026-07-14 · 6 min read

Whoever organises a trip for their parish or their club usually does not do it for a living: they do it because someone had to. Here is the order of things, learned over ten years of groups.

The number first, the destination second. It sounds like the opposite of what people usually do, and that is exactly the point. Half the cost of a group trip is fixed — the coach, the guide, the tour leader — and is divided among the participants. Twenty people pay much more than forty for the same programme. Before dreaming of the destination, look at how many of you are really willing to go.

Ask for a written quote, with the minimum in it. A serious quote says how many people are needed for that price to stand up. If it is not written there, ask for it: that is the difference between a price and a promise.

Block the services before opening bookings. Hotel rooms for groups are held on option, with a deadline. Opening bookings without having the rooms is the mistake that costs the most: if the hotel then fills up, you have to change the programme for people who have already paid a deposit.

Always take a deposit. Without a deposit the booking does not exist: people change their minds, and meanwhile the coach is committed. A deposit of twenty-five per cent on booking is the norm, and it also tells you whether the group is really there.

Ask for the balance thirty days ahead. Not the day before departure: by then the agency has already paid the hotels, and you have no room to manoeuvre.

Check the documents early. Expired identity cards and passports with less than six months left are discovered at the airport, when it is too late. An agency that works properly asks for them two months ahead and checks them.
