How it works

From the first phone call to departure, four steps. None of them costs anything until you decide.

  1. 1

    You tell us where and when

    Three things are enough: where you would like to go, roughly when, and roughly how many you are.

  2. 2

    We send you the quote

    Within two working days: day-by-day programme, price per person and what it includes. In writing, not over the phone.

  3. 3

    Bookings open

    We give you the poster to put up and the booking form. We keep track of seats and documents.

  4. 4

    Off you go

    Rooms assigned, coach seats assigned, roll call at departure. You just get on board.
