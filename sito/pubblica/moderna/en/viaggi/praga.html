Five days between the old town and the castles

New Year in Prague

Charles Bridge, the Castle, Karlovy Vary and New Year's Eve dinner with fireworks over the Vltava.

5days
4nights
€ 690per person in a double room
30minimum to depart

Prague at New Year is something people talk about for years: the old town in lights, the astronomical clock, the markets that hold on into January and New Year's Eve on Charles Bridge. You leave in the evening by coach with two drivers, sleep in a four-star hotel with the metro at the door, and the gala dinner is included, drinks and all.

Day by day

  1. D. 1
    Milan

    overnight journey: evening meeting point and departure, with stops along the way.

  2. D. 2
    Prague, the old town: Old Town Square, the Jewish quarter and Charles Bridge with the guide.
  3. D. 3
    Castle and Malá Strana: St Vitus, the Golden Lane, and in the evening the New Year's Eve dinner.
  4. D. 4
    Karlovy Vary: the colonnades, the hot springs and a free afternoon in the spa town.
  5. D. 5
    Home: a last free morning and departure, back in Milan late in the evening.

What the price includes

  • Coach with two drivers
  • 3 nights in a four-star hotel on half board
  • New Year's Eve gala dinner with drinks included
  • Local guide for two days, with radio headsets
  • Excursion to Karlovy Vary
  • Tour leader from Milan
  • Medical and baggage insurance

What it does not include

  • Lunches
  • Entrance fees to monuments (about €25)
  • City tax
  • Cancellation insurance (optional)
