Morocco — the Imperial Cities

Eight days across the four imperial cities, with a scheduled flight from Milan and an Italian-speaking guide.

8days
7nights
€ 1090per person in a double room
25minimum to depart

The Morocco you expect and the one that surprises you: the mosque on the ocean in Casablanca, the walls of Meknes, the maze of Fez — the largest medieval old town in the world — and Marrakech, where at sunset the square turns into a theatre. You sleep in four-star hotels and riads on full board, and a local agent follows the group every day.

Day by day

  1. D. 1
    Milan

    Casablanca: scheduled flight, transfer to the hotel and dinner.

  2. D. 2
    Casablanca and Rabat: the Hassan II mosque on the Atlantic, then the Oudayas Kasbah and the Mausoleum.
  3. D. 3
    Meknes and Volubilis: Bab Mansour, the royal stables and the Roman site among the olive trees. On to Fez.
  4. D. 4
    Fez: a full day in the medina, the Bou Inania madrasa and the tanneries. Typical dinner with a show.
  5. D. 5
    Fez

    Marrakech: the Middle Atlas, the cedar forest of Azrou and the monkeys along the road.

  6. D. 6
    Marrakech: Koutoubia, Saadian tombs, Bahia palace and Majorelle gardens. In the evening, Jemaa el-Fna.
  7. D. 7
    Free day or optional excursion to Essaouira on the ocean.
  8. D. 8
    Home: transfer to the airport and flight to Milan.

What the price includes

  • Return scheduled flight from Milan with taxes
  • Coach for all transfers in Morocco
  • 7 nights in four-star hotels and riads, full board
  • Italian-speaking local guide throughout
  • Entrance fees to the monuments in the programme
  • Typical dinner with a show in Fez
  • Tour leader from Italy
  • Medical and baggage insurance

What it does not include

  • Drinks with meals
  • Tips (about €30 per person, collected locally)
  • Optional excursion to Essaouira
  • Cancellation insurance (optional)
