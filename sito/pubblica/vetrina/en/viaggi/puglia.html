Six days of trulli, baroque and cave dwellings

Apulia and Matera

Alberobello, Matera, Lecce, Otranto, Ostuni and Gallipoli, with tastings at country estates.

6days
5nights
€ 740per person in a double room
30minimum to depart

Apulia works well by coach, and in six days it all fits together: the trulli of Alberobello, the Sassi of Matera with a guide who tells you what it was like before the evacuation, the baroque of Lecce, the mosaic of Otranto and the white town of Ostuni. In between, two tastings at masserie that are worth the trip on their own.

Day by day

  1. D. 1
    Milan

    Apulia: coach journey with stops, evening arrival and dinner at the hotel.

  2. D. 2
    Alberobello and the Itria valley: the trulli, Locorotondo and Martina Franca.
  3. D. 3
    Matera: the Sassi with a guide, the Cave House and the cathedral.
  4. D. 4
    Lecce and Otranto: Santa Croce, the cathedral square and the cathedral mosaic.
  5. D. 5
    Ostuni and Gallipoli: the white town, the old town on the sea and a tasting at a masseria.
  6. D. 6
    Home: departure after breakfast and back in Milan in the evening.

What the price includes

  • Coach for the whole trip
  • 5 nights in four-star hotels on half board
  • Two tastings at country estates and a typical lunch
  • Local guides in Alberobello, Matera and Lecce
  • Entrance fees in the programme
  • Tour leader from Milan
  • Medical and baggage insurance

What it does not include

  • Meals not listed and drinks
  • Tips
  • Cancellation insurance (optional)
