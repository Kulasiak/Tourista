Pilgrimage to Lourdes

Coach travel, full board with drinks, and the right amount of time for the celebrations.

This is the trip we have been running longest, and we have adjusted it pilgrimage after pilgrimage. The hotel is three hundred metres from the Grotto, so those who walk slowly do not need shuttles; board is full and drinks are included; the programme leaves room for the Way of the Cross, the torchlight procession and personal prayer. The coach has a wheelchair lift — just tell us when you book.

Day by day

  1. D. 1
    Early morning departure, stops along the way and arrival in Lourdes in the evening.
  2. D. 2
    The Sanctuary: the Grotto of Massabielle, the basilicas, the Way of the Cross and the torchlight procession.
  3. D. 3
    Mass at the Grotto, time for the water gesture and the Blessed Sacrament procession in the afternoon.
  4. D. 4
    Bernadette's places: the Cachot, the Boly Mill and Bartrès. Free afternoon.
  5. D. 5
    Home after breakfast, with a stop along the way.

What the price includes

  • Coach with wheelchair lift
  • 4 nights in a hotel 300 m from the Grotto
  • Full board with drinks at meals
  • Tour leader and assistance throughout
  • City tax
  • Medical and baggage insurance

What it does not include

  • Lunches on the way there and back
