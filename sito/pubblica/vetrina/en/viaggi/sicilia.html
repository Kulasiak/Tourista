Eight days, a whole region

Grand Tour of Sicily

Palermo, Erice, Agrigento, Piazza Armerina, Syracuse, Taormina and Mount Etna, by coach from Italy.

8days
7nights
€ 890per person in a double room
30minimum to depart

This is our most requested trip, and it is easy to see why: in eight days you see the real Sicily, the one of temples and mosaics, of markets and volcanoes. You travel by coach with an overnight ferry on the way out, sleep in four-star hotels on half board and eat where Sicilians eat. Licensed local guides in every city, with radio headsets so that even the back of the group hears well.

Day by day

  1. D. 1
    Milan

    Naples — boarding: early morning departure, boarding in the afternoon and reserved cabins.

  2. D. 2
    Palermo and Monreale: the cathedral with its mosaics, the cloister, the Quattro Canti and the Vucciria market.
  3. D. 3
    Erice and Segesta: the medieval hill town and the Doric temple among the hills. Trapani couscous for lunch.
  4. D. 4
    Valley of the Temples: a guided visit of the archaeological park, and in the evening the temples lit up from the hotel.
  5. D. 5
    Piazza Armerina and Syracuse: the mosaics of Villa del Casale, then Ortigia and the Arethusa spring.
  6. D. 6
    Etna and Taormina: up to the Silvestri craters at 1,900 metres, then the Greek Theatre and Corso Umberto.
  7. D. 7
    Catania and boarding: the fish market, Piazza Duomo and Via Etnea before the journey home.
  8. D. 8
    Home: morning arrival in port and back in Milan in the evening.

What the price includes

  • Coach for the whole trip, with two drivers
  • Return ferry with reserved cabins
  • 7 nights in four-star hotels on half board
  • 2 lunches in restaurants and a typical dinner in Taormina
  • Licensed local guides with radio headsets
  • Entrance fees: Monreale, Valley of the Temples, Villa del Casale, Greek Theatre
  • Tour leader from Milan
  • Medical and baggage insurance

What it does not include

  • Meals not listed and drinks with meals
  • Tips and porterage
  • Cancellation insurance (optional, €45)
  • Anything not written above
