If the group is there, the trip can be built

We do not sell packages: we build the trip around the group that is travelling. How many you are, what pace you keep, what you can spend, what must not be missing. Then we send you a written quote with everything in it.

Parishes and pilgrimages

Lourdes, Fatima, Assisi, the Holy Land, Medjugorje and the shrines nearby. Hotels close to the holy places, time for the celebrations, a coach with a wheelchair lift when needed. We invoice the parish and accept a single bank transfer.

  • Programme of celebrations agreed with the parish priest
  • Ground-floor rooms for those who struggle with stairs
  • One free place for every twenty paying

Schools and study trips

A formal quote for the tender, with all the paperwork: licence, liability insurance, guarantee fund and the declarations the call for tenders asks for. Teachers in single rooms at no supplement and free places according to the number of students.

  • Three comparable quotes for the school board
  • One free place for every fifteen students
  • Accident insurance and 24-hour assistance

Staff clubs, companies and circles

An agreement with reduced prices for members, a poster customised with your logo and a single contact who follows the bookings. Invoice to the club or individual payments, as you prefer.

  • Agreement discount up to 5%
  • Poster and ready-made texts to send to members
  • Bookings handled by us, no work for you

Associations, clubs and groups of friends

A gentle pace, comfortable rooms, assigned coach seats and a tour leader with patience. Small groups too: from twenty-five participants we run a dedicated coach.

  • From 25 participants upwards
  • Coach seats assigned before departure
  • Diets and allergies sent to the hotels

The guarantees, in writing

Licence and insurance

Provincial authorisation, professional liability insurance and the guarantee fund required by the Italian Tourism Code: the numbers are at the bottom of every page.

The price is locked

The price written on the quote is the price you pay, unless the number of participants changes and we agree it with you.

Deposit and balance

Deposit on booking, balance thirty days before departure. A receipt for every payment, always.

If the group does not fill up

We tell you at least forty-five days ahead and decide together: postpone, change transport, or give everything back.

What we need to start

Three pieces of information: where, when, how many. We will ask for the rest.

Let us talk

Write us a couple of lines with the idea. We will call you back, at the time you tell us.
