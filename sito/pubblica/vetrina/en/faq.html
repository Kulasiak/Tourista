The questions we are asked most

If yours is not here, write to us: we answer within the working day and add the question to this page.

How much do you pay on booking?

A deposit, usually 25% of the price (for example €250 on a trip of €890). The balance is paid thirty days before departure. We issue a receipt for every payment.

What happens if the group does not reach the minimum?

Every trip has a minimum number of participants written on the programme. If we do not reach it we tell you at least forty-five days ahead and decide together: move the date, change the transport with an agreed adjustment, or give back everything that has been paid.

Is insurance included?

Medical and baggage insurance is always included in the price. Cancellation insurance is optional and is added when you book: it costs about €45 and covers withdrawal for documented reasons, illness included.

What documents are needed?

For Italy and the European Union a valid identity card for travel abroad is enough. For Morocco and Tunisia you need a passport valid for at least six months beyond your return. We check the expiry dates before departure and warn you in good time.

Can I have a single room?

Yes, with a supplement written on every programme (usually between €120 and €290 for the whole trip). Hotels have few single rooms: it is best to ask when booking.

I am travelling alone: will you find me a roommate?

We always try, and most of the time we succeed. If we find nobody, the single supplement still applies, but we tell you before the balance is due, not at the last minute.

Does the coach make stops?

About every two hours, and whenever needed. On long journeys there are two drivers, as the law requires. We assign the coach seats before departure: whoever suffers from travel sickness sits at the front.

Are there barriers for people who struggle to walk?

It depends on the trip, and we write it on the programme. For Lourdes the coach has a wheelchair lift and the hotel is three hundred metres from the Grotto. On other trips there are stairways that cannot be avoided: we tell you before, not after.

What about diets and allergies?

You tell us when booking and we pass them to the hotels and restaurants before departure, in writing. Coeliac disease, lactose intolerance, vegetarian and vegan are handled without trouble almost everywhere.

How do you pay?

Bank transfer, card or cash within the legal limits. For parishes, schools and clubs we invoice the body with a single transfer. We never ask for payments into accounts other than the one written on our documents.

How much does a quote cost?

Nothing. The quote is free and commits you to nothing: we send it within two working days, with the day-by-day programme and the price for the exact number of people in your group.

What guarantees do you have?

Provincial authorisation no. 442 of 2016, professional liability insurance and the guarantee fund required by article 47 of the Italian Tourism Code. The references are at the bottom of every page of this site and on every contract.

Can we have a trip just for our group?

That is what we do most often. From twenty-five participants upwards we build the trip to measure, on the dates you choose, with the programme you prefer.

Who accompanies the group?

A tour leader from our agency, who leaves Milan with you and comes back with you. They are not a local guide: they are the person who keeps the group together, talks to the hotels and solves problems. Licensed local guides come in addition, in the cities where they are needed.
